TJR Poster

TJR Poster is a private tool that its owner uses to upload short videos to their own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TJR, Google or YouTube.

Why it asks for Google access

  • To upload videos and their thumbnails to the owner's channels.
  • To read the channel name, so uploads go to the right channel.
  • If the owner separately allows it, to read and reply to comments on those channels.
