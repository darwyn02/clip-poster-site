TJR Poster privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

TJR Poster is a private tool used by its owner on their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services, which are covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, it uploads videos and thumbnails, and reads basic channel and video information. If separately allowed, it reads and replies to comments on the owner's channels.

What it stores

Google sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's computer and as secrets in the owner's private GitHub Actions setup. Videos waiting to be posted are kept in the owner's Cloudflare R2 storage. Posting logs record video links and results.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. It is handled only by the services needed to run the tool (Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, notification services that receive posting results, and AI assistant software the owner uses to operate it).

Removal

Access can be removed at any time in Google account permissions. Stored tokens can then be deleted by the owner.
